# Product quality filter

Use for UI/frontend/product delivery.

Do not prescribe a house style. Check instead for:
- dead controls;
- fake data or fabricated product claims;
- broken responsive behavior;
- missing interaction states;
- avoidable accessibility failures;
- unnecessary decorative complexity;
- incomplete or obviously placeholder implementation.

The user's design direction remains authoritative.
